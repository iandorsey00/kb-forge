## Symptoms

The room display is on, but shows **No signal** after a laptop is connected.

## Procedure

1. Wake the laptop and confirm the cable is seated at both ends.
2. Open the display's input menu. Choose the HDMI input printed beside the cable port.
3. Wait for the laptop desktop to appear.

![Input selector with HDMI 1 selected](assets/input-selector.png)

| Screen message | Next check |
| --- | --- |
| No signal | Confirm the input number and reseat the cable. |
| Unsupported format | Check the laptop's display resolution. |

## Verification

Confirm the laptop desktop appears and stays visible for at least 30 seconds.

For loaner equipment, see the [North America addendum](../FIELD-000002/en-US.md).
